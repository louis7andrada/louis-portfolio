Five Business Days
A Screenplay
by Han’drada, 2026
https://han.andrada.one/oeuvre/five-business-days/

[page 1]
The first half of this story happened. On the evening of the nineteenth of October, in a small restaurant on the slope of Montmartre, a man looked at a woman at the next table, and she looked back, and a telephone that should have rung did not.
The second half had not happened when these pages were written. They were written a few hours later, in a rented apartment in the same city, on a sofa bed, quietly, so as not to wake his parents, by a man whose phone was finally connected and whose request for a list of calls was waiting, unopened, in an inbox on the other side of the ocean.
So this is a film made of two halves: one that was lived and one that was hoped. The reader may decide where the seam runs. It has been left visible on purpose. A good repair always is.
Han’drada
